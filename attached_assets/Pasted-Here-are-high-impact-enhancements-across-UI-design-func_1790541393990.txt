Here are high-impact enhancements across UI design, functional logic, and usability that will elevate **FitPulse** to a polished, premium fitness platform:

---

### 1. Unified App-Wide State & Auto-Sync

* **Target-to-Dashboard Sync:** When clicking **Apply these targets** in *My Plan*, immediately update the macro limits and calorie goals on both the *Nutrition* screen and the *Today* dashboard.


* **Shared Body Weight Metric:** Dynamically share the body weight value entered in *My Plan* ($75\text{ kg}$) with the *Movement* screen's calorie burn formula so users don't have to input weight twice.


* **Live Step Progress Bar:** Reflect real-time steps added in *Movement* directly onto the *Today* dashboard's progress ring/cards.



---

### 2. Strength Room Upgrades

* **Dynamic Warm-Up & Working Set Tags:** Add set-type selectors (Warm-up, Working, Drop set, Failure) alongside weight and reps.
* **Auto-Rest Timer Header:** Trigger an overlay rest timer (e.g., $90\text{s}$ or $180\text{s}$ countdown with chime) as soon as a set is logged.
* **Personal Best (PR) Badges:** Display a subtle gold badge or visual highlight next to logged sets when an estimated 1RM beats a previous personal record.


* **Quick Load Presets:** Store recent exercises in a quick-select carousel so users don't have to type exercise names manually every time.

---

### 3. Advanced Pedometer & Motion Feedback

* **Live Sensor Visualizer:** Replace the static "Start live tracking" card with an animated pulse ring or wave indicator when step tracking is actively running on mobile devices.


* **Hourly Activity Bar Charts:** In the *Your week in motion* card, add a toggle to switch between daily totals and hourly breakdowns (to show peak active hours during the day).



---

### 4. Nutrition & Meal Experience

* **Macro Distribution Bars:** Add visual progress meters for Protein, Carbs, and Fats right under the logged meal lists.


* **Quick Meal Templates / Favorites:** Allow saving custom recurring meals (e.g., "Post-Workout Shake" or "Standard Breakfast") for one-tap logging.
* **Water Intake Tracker:** Add a minimal quick-add water logger ($250\text{ ml}$ increment buttons) inside the *Nutrition* tab.
